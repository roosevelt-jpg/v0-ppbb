VOLUME 23 — VERBALAB AI ECONOMY

Mission: build a marketplace/commerce platform for AI products, services and data — developer & partner onboarding, licensing, subscriptions, usage billing, revenue sharing/payouts, a talent marketplace, grant/funding tracking, and an "investment platform" and economic-analytics dashboards. Build this as VerbaLab's own internal commerce/marketplace platform infrastructure, not as a claim that it is already "the world's largest AI economy" — that status, if it ever happens, comes from external adoption over years, not from this code existing.

IMPORTANT — read before building: phases in this volume name real money movement (billing, licensing, royalties, revenue sharing, payouts) and, in Phase 248, an "Investment Platform." Treat anything that touches real payments, payouts, or investment the same way Volume 11 (Monetization Cloud) was treated: build the software, but do not let Cursor wire up real money movement, real securities/investment transactions, or real tax/1099-style reporting without a human (ideally with legal/finance/compliance sign-off) in the loop. If an "Investment Platform" phase is interpreted literally — letting users actually invest money in startups/research through this product — that is a securities-regulation question (equivalent to running a funding portal or broker-dealer in many jurisdictions), not something to resolve by writing more code.
