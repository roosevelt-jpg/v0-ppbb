# BUSINESS DASHBOARD - COMPLETE FEATURE WALKTHROUGH

## Overview
The Business Dashboard is a comprehensive portal for business users to manage opportunities, offers, leads, referrals, partnerships, and payments. It provides complete business management tools integrated with the Passive Blessings community platform.

---

## 1. BUSINESS DASHBOARD (Main Homepage)

### Key Features:
- **Welcome Header**: Personalized greeting with business name
- **Quick Stats Grid** (4 main metrics):
  - Opportunities Posted (with count of open opportunities)
  - Offers Posted (products, services, discounts)
  - Leads Generated (with conversion rate %)
  - Referral Earnings (with pending commission amount)

- **Secondary Stats** (3 additional metrics):
  - Active Partnerships (collaboration count)
  - Business Rating (average 5-star rating)
  - Completed Payments (completed vs total payments)

- **Quick Actions Section** (4 buttons):
  - Post Opportunity
  - Post Offer
  - Request Partnership
  - Edit Profile

- **Business Management Grid** (9 navigation cards):
  - Opportunities: Manage jobs, internships, and gigs
  - Offers: Post products, services, and discounts
  - Leads: Track and manage customer leads
  - Referrals: Monitor commission earnings
  - Partnerships: Manage business collaborations
  - Analytics: View performance metrics
  - Marketplace: Connect with community members
  - Payments: Manage subscriptions and payouts
  - Settings: Configure business profile

---

## 2. OPPORTUNITIES MANAGEMENT
- **Types**: Jobs, internships, gigs
- **Operations**: Create, view, edit, delete
- **Tracking**: Open vs closed opportunities
- **Analytics**: Leads per opportunity

---

## 3. OFFERS MANAGEMENT
- **Types**: Products, services, discounts, promotions
- **Management**: Post, edit, delete offers
- **Display**: Title, description, type, price, validity
- **Analytics**: Active offers, redemption rate

---

## 4. LEADS TRACKER
- **Statistics**: Total leads, converted, conversion rate %
- **Filtering**: All, new, in progress, contacted, converted, lost
- **Actions**: Update status, add notes, export data
- **Details**: Name, contact, source, status history

---

## 5. REFERRALS & COMMISSIONS
- **Earnings**: Total referral earnings, pending commission
- **Tracking**: Referral links, conversions, structure
- **Performance**: Top performers, conversion rates, trends

---

## 6. PARTNERSHIPS
- **Management**: View, request, manage partnerships
- **Types**: Collaborations, cross-promotions, joint ventures, affiliates
- **Metrics**: Active count, performance, benefits

---

## 7. BUSINESS ANALYTICS
- **Metrics**: Opportunities, offers, leads, conversion rate

## 8. PAYMENTS & SUBSCRIPTIONS
