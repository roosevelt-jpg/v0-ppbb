# Justice Language Access

Help courts and legal aid **defend people who struggle due to language barriers**.

## What it does

1. Ingest testimony in the speaker’s language
2. Build a dual-language defense brief (speaker ↔ court language)
3. Produce plain-language rights summaries (KE/ZA/NG templates + translate)

## Honesty

Assistive language access — **not legal advice** and not a replacement for counsel or sworn interpreters where required.

## Endpoints

`POST /v1/justice-language-access/ingest|brief|rights` · `GET /v1/justice-language-access/cases`

## Console

`/justice-language-access`
