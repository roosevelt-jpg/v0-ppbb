# Oral-First Knowledge OS

Ingest radio, WhatsApp voice notes, market chatter, elders’ speech into citeable oral knowledge without forcing literacy first.

## APIs

- **Oral ingest from audio/text**: `POST /v1/oral-knowledge/ingest`
- **Cite back to oral source**: `POST /v1/oral-knowledge/cite`
- **Ask oral knowledge**: `POST /v1/oral-knowledge/query`
- **Community collections**: `GET /v1/oral-knowledge/collections`

Also: `GET /v1/oral-knowledge/engine`, `/overview`, `/activity`, `/monitoring`.
