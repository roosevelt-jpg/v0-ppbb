# Civic Truth Guard

Help prevent **fake news** and **deepfake voice** online by assessing claims, verifying Civic Voice Seals, and publishing authenticity reports.

## Honesty

Integrity assistant for citizens, newsrooms, and civic orgs — **not** an automatic takedown authority or ministry of truth.

## Endpoints

`POST /v1/civic-truth-guard/assess|verify-seal|report` · `GET /v1/civic-truth-guard/signals`

## Console

`/civic-truth-guard`
