This would be a secure platform for organizations to exchange AI-ready data under governed policies.

Capabilities

Federated datasets

Data clean rooms
Differential privacy
Federated learning

This would allow:

Banks to build fraud models across institutions while preserving confidentiality.
Governments to share multilingual datasets under controlled agreements.
Universities to contribute research datasets with clear licensing.

An AI Data Exchange complements the AI Economy by making data—not just models and applications—a managed, governed, and monetizable asset.
